Mind Forge – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mind Forge, an educational training app designed for children and parents to enhance learning and cognitive development. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you use our application.

Mind Forge is developed by Mind Forge Development and is distributed via Google Play (Internal Testing). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using Mind Forge, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Mind Forge collects only the minimal information necessary to provide and improve our educational services:

  • Account Information: When you create an account using Firebase Authentication, we collect basic authentication data such as email addresses or authentication tokens provided by third-party sign-in services (e.g., Google Sign-In).
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the app, including learning progress, session data, and activity completion status. This data is stored in Firebase Firestore to help track educational achievements and personalize the learning experience.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version to ensure compatibility and optimize performance.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: If enabled, we may use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information to identify and fix technical issues. This data is anonymized and used solely for app stability improvements.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your privacy and do not collect the following types of information:

  • Location data (GPS, precise or approximate location)
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Photos, videos, or media files from your device
  • SMS messages or call logs
  • Financial or payment information
  • Microphone or camera access (unless explicitly requested for specific educational features, with your permission)

Note: Mind Forge does not display advertisements in its initial version, and we do not collect data for advertising purposes.

4. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Provide Educational Services: To deliver personalized learning experiences, track progress, and save session data.
  • User Authentication: To securely authenticate users and manage accounts via Firebase Authentication.
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, identify technical issues, and improve app functionality and user experience.
  • Communication: To respond to user inquiries, provide support, and send important service-related notifications (e.g., policy updates).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Mind Forge uses Firebase (a Google service) to provide backend infrastructure. This includes:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure user sign-in and account management.
  • Firebase Firestore: For storing user progress, session data, and educational achievements.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: For crash reporting and diagnostic analysis (optional).

Firebase processes data in accordance with Google's privacy policies. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

We do not share your data with any other third parties except as required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Children's Privacy

Mind Forge is designed for use by children under the age of 13, with parental supervision. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

Parental Consent: Before a child can use Mind Forge, we require verifiable parental consent. Parents or guardians must create and manage accounts on behalf of their children.

Data Minimization: We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide educational services. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.

No Behavioral Advertising: Mind Forge does not display advertisements or use children's data for behavioral advertising purposes.

Parental Rights: Parents have the right to:

  • Review the personal information collected from their child
  • Request deletion of their child's personal information
  • Refuse further collection or use of their child's information

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@mindforgeapp.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Account Data: Retained as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data will be deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
  • Usage and Session Data: Progress and session data are retained to provide continuous learning experiences. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Crash Reports: Anonymized crash data is retained for up to 90 days for diagnostic purposes.

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymized so that it can no longer be associated with individual users.

8. Security

We take the security of your information seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect it:

  • Encryption: Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using secure protocols (SSL/TLS).
  • Firebase Security: User data stored in Firebase Firestore is protected by Firebase security rules and access controls.
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication uses secure, industry-standard methods to verify user identities.
  • Regular Monitoring: We regularly monitor our systems for potential vulnerabilities and security threats.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to using reasonable safeguards.

9. User Rights (Access and Deletion)

You have the right to access, modify, and delete your personal information:

How to Request Data Access or Deletion:

  • In-App: You can access and manage your data through the app's account settings. To delete your account and all associated data, navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Email Request: You may also request data access or deletion by contacting us at support@mindforgeapp.com. Please include your account email and specify your request (access or deletion).
  • Response Time: We will respond to your request within 30 days and complete data deletion within 30 days of verification.

Please note that some information may be retained for legal or security purposes as permitted by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or app features. When we make significant changes, we will notify you by:

  • Posting a notice in the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending an email notification (if applicable)

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Continued use of Mind Forge after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
