Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mind Forge, a learning application designed to enhance your educational experience. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using Mind Forge, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Mind Forge collects limited information necessary to provide and improve our services. We use Firebase services to manage our backend infrastructure:

2.1 Authentication Data (Firebase Authentication)

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and login
  • User ID: A unique identifier assigned to your account
  • Authentication Tokens: Used to maintain your session securely

2.2 Application Data (Cloud Firestore)

  • Learning Progress: Your course progress, completed lessons, and achievements
  • User Preferences: App settings and customization options
  • Profile Information: Display name and any optional information you provide
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app features

2.3 Crash and Performance Data (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version
  • Crash Reports: Technical data about app crashes and errors
  • Performance Metrics: App loading times and performance data
  • Anonymous Identifiers: Used to track crashes and performance issues

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect. Mind Forge does NOT access, collect, or store:

  • Location Data: We do not track your physical location
  • Contacts: We do not access your contact list
  • Photos or Media: We do not access your photos, videos, or media files
  • Messages: We do not read or collect your SMS, MMS, or other messages
  • Financial Information: We do not collect credit card numbers, bank account information, or other financial data
  • Personal Documents: We do not access files stored on your device
  • Microphone or Camera: We do not use your device's camera or microphone

No Advertising: We do not display advertisements in Mind Forge, and we do not collect data for advertising purposes.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and maintain your user account
  • Service Delivery: To provide learning content and track your progress
  • Personalization: To customize your learning experience based on your preferences
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality
  • Technical Support: To diagnose and fix technical issues
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access and ensure data security
  • Communication: To send important updates about the app or your account

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Mind Forge uses Firebase, a platform provided by Google LLC, to handle our backend services:

5.1 Firebase Services

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages user accounts and authentication
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores your learning data and preferences
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports and performance data

These services are subject to Google's privacy policies. Firebase data is stored on Google's secure servers and is protected according to industry standards.

5.2 No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with Firebase/Google as necessary to provide our services.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of users.

6. Children's Privacy

Mind Forge is designed as a learning application that may be used by children under the age of 13 with parental consent. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our educational services
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent
  • Parents can review their child's information and request deletion at any time
  • We do not display advertising or collect data for behavioral advertising

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at support@mindforgeapp.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: After account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days
  • Crash Reports: Retained for up to 90 days to diagnose and fix issues
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

8. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols
  • Firebase Security: Data is stored on Firebase's secure infrastructure with industry-standard protections
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Updates: We keep our security practices and software up to date

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: You can update or correct your information through the app settings
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You can withdraw consent for data processing at any time

9.1 How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account and associated data:

  1. Open the Mind Forge app and go to Settings
  2. Select "Account Settings"
  3. Choose "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm your decision

Alternatively, you can email us at support@mindforgeapp.com to request account deletion.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • We will notify you through the app or via email for significant changes
  • Your continued use of Mind Forge after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
