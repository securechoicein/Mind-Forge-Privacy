Account & Data Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your Mind Forge account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

Option 1 (In-App):

Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Option 2 (Email Request):

Email us at:

support@mindforgeapp.com

Please include the account email used in the app.

All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of verification.
